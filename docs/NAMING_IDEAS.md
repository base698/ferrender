# Naming Ideas

Ferrender title, subtitle, and product-name ideas inspired by creation through speech: words becoming form, thoughts becoming real, and worlds being spoken into existence.

Brainstorm from October 8, 2026. The preferred phrases are **Words become worlds**, followed by **Into being**. These are the user's favorites; the alternative names and pairings below remain exploratory.

## Preferred Ferrender pairings

1. **Ferrender — Words become worlds.** Memorable and expansive, with a strong connection between language and visual creation.
2. **Ferrender — Into being.** Quieter, minimal, and focused on the moment an idea becomes real.

## English naming vocabulary

| --- | --- |

| Invocation | Calling something into presence; ritual and intention. |
| Utterance | Speech with weight and consequence. |

| Worldspoken | Coined name suggesting a world brought into being through speech. |

## Hebrew Greek and Japanese names

Pronunciations are approximate English guides. The naming impressions are creative interpretations of the sourced meanings.

| Name | Language and pronunciation | Meaning and naming connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kotodama** · 言霊 | Japanese · ko-to-da-ma | “Spirit of words.” The belief that words possess spiritual power and can affect events makes this the closest conceptual match to “words become worlds.” Mythic and memorable. [Kokugakuin University](https://d-museum.kokugakuin.ac.jp/eos/detail/?id=8660) |
| **Naru** · 成る | Japanese · na-roo | To become; to come to fruition. Short and soft, with a close connection to “into being.” [Japanese dictionary](https://dictionary.goo.ne.jp/word/成る/) |
| **Yehi** · יְהִי | Hebrew · yeh-HEE | “Let there be,” as in *yehi or*, “let there be light.” A small name with a direct creation-story connection. [Genesis 1:3](https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.1.3) |
| **Davar** · דָּבָר | Hebrew · dah-VAHR | Word, matter, or thing, depending on context. That overlap makes it especially fitting for the concept of words becoming worlds. Also transliterated *dabar*. [Hebrew lexicon](https://www.blueletterbible.org/lexicon/h1697/esv/) |
| **Bara** · בָּרָא | Hebrew · bah-RAH | To create; the creation verb used in Genesis. Simple and elemental, with a distinctly divine association in its biblical creation usage. [Hebrew lexicon](https://blueletter.bible/lexicon/h1254/nasb20/wlc/) |
| **Rhema** · ῥῆμα | Greek · RAY-mah | An utterance; something spoken. Elegant and compact, with a direct connection to language. [Greek lexicon](https://biblehub.com/greek/4487.htm) |
| **Poiesis** · ποίησις | Greek · poy-AY-sis | Making, creation, production. Artistic and philosophical; a strong fit for bringing ideas into form. [Greek lexicon](https://atlas.perseus.tufts.edu/dictionaries/headword/ποίησις/) |
| **Logos** · λόγος | Greek · LOG-oss | Word, speech, reason, or account. Its use as the divine “Word” gives it a creation association. [Greek lexicon](https://biblehub.com/greek/3056.htm) |

## Suggested alternative name pairings

These were the assistant's suggested combinations, rather than selected names:

- **Naru — Words become worlds.** The cleanest, softest pairing.
- **Rhema — Words become worlds.** More weight and mystery.
- **Yehi — Into being.** Minimal, with a direct creation-story connection.

**Kotodama** offers the richest conceptual story. **Naru** was the assistant's favorite on sound alone. **Words become worlds** remains the user's strongest preference for the subtitle.
